Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is handled in Huge Clock (the "App").

1. Data Collection and Usage

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information from our users. The App functions locally on your device. We do not require you to create an account, nor do we track your location.

2. Third-Party Services (Ads)

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob is an advertising service provided by Google Inc.

AdMob may collect and use generic data (such as Advertising ID, device type, and approximate location based on IP address) to serve personalized ads. This data collection is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob & Privacy

3. Device Permissions

The App requests specific permissions to function correctly:

  • Prevent phone from sleeping (WAKE_LOCK): Used solely to keep the timer or clock visible without the screen turning off automatically.
  • Change system settings (WRITE_SETTINGS): Used only to adjust screen brightness to maximum when you enable the "Full Brightness" mode in the timer. We do not change any other settings.

4. Children’s Privacy

Although our App is safe for all ages, we do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
