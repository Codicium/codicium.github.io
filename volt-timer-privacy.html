Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the following application developed by Codicium:

Volt Timer

1. Overview

Codicium respects your privacy. Our apps are designed to be as private as possible. We do not require account registration, and we do not collect, store, or share any of your personally identifiable information (PII).

2. Data Collection and Use

We do not collect any personal data such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location. All application settings, custom workouts, and preferences are stored locally on your device.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. These services include:

  • Google AdMob: Used to provide advertisements. AdMob may use device identifiers to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. View Google's Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Services: Used for in-app purchases and basic app functionality. View Google's Privacy Policy

4. Permissions

The App may request the following permissions to function correctly:

  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE: To keep a workout's lock-screen controls active while the app runs in the background.
  • WAKE_LOCK: To keep the screen or CPU awake during an active workout.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: To show workout progress and pause/resume/stop controls in your device's notification area.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: To restore any active workout after your device reboots.
  • VIBRATE: To provide haptic feedback on phase changes (work/rest/round transitions).
  • INTERNET: To load advertisements and handle in-app purchase verification.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING: To facilitate in-app purchases.

5. Children's Privacy

Our App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided personal data, please contact us.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at codicium91+dev@gmail.com.
