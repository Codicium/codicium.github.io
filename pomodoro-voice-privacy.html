Privacy Policy for Pomodoro Voice

Last updated: January 16, 2026

Pomodoro Voice ("the App") is developed as a focused productivity tool. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

1. Data Collection and Use

We do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information. All settings and timer data are stored locally on your device.

2. Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google AdMob (Advertising)

3. Permissions

The App requests the following permissions to function correctly:

  • WAKE_LOCK: To keep the screen on during focus sessions (if enabled).
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: To show timer progress and controls in the notification bar.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: To ensure accurate timer transitions.
