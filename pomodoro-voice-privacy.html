Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the following applications developed by Codicium:

Pomodoro Voice Huge Clock

1. Overview

Codicium respects your privacy. Our apps are designed to be as private as possible. We do not require account registration, and we do not collect, store, or share any of your personally identifiable information (PII).

2. Data Collection and Use

We do not collect any personal data such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location. All application settings, timer data, and preferences are stored locally on your device.

3. Third-Party Services

The Apps use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. These services include:

  • Google AdMob: Used to provide advertisements. AdMob may use device identifiers to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. View Google's Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Services: Used for in-app purchases and basic app functionality. View Google's Privacy Policy

4. Permissions

The Apps may request the following permissions to function correctly:

  • WAKE_LOCK: To keep the screen or CPU awake during active timers or clock display.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: To show timer progress and controls in your device's notification area.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: To ensure timers and alarms trigger at the precise time.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: To restore any active timers after your device reboots.
  • INTERNET: To load advertisements and handle in-app purchase verification.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING: To facilitate in-app purchases.

5. Children's Privacy

Our Apps do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided personal data, please contact us.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at codicium91+dev@gmail.com.
