Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the following application developed by Codicium:

Pomodoro Voice

1. Overview

Codicium respects your privacy. Pomodoro Voice has no account system and no server of our own: we do not ask for your name, email address or any other personal details, and nothing you do in the app is sent to us. The only data that leaves your device is collected by the Google services described below.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Your settings, timer progress, and the fact that you have removed ads or unlocked a voice pack are stored locally on your device only. We do not receive this information, and we do not collect your precise location.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following Google services. Google, as the provider of these services, receives the data they collect and handles it under its own policies:

  • Google AdMob: Used to show advertisements. AdMob may collect device and advertising identifiers, your approximate location (derived from your IP address) and information about how you interact with ads and the app, in order to serve and measure ads, and to personalize them where you have allowed it. View Google's Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google Firebase Analytics: Used to understand, in aggregate, how the App is used (for example which screens are opened, whether a focus session is completed, and whether a purchase is started and completed). It may collect device identifiers, app interactions and approximate location derived from your IP address. We cannot identify you from this data. View Google's Privacy Policy · Privacy and security in Firebase
  • Google Play (Billing): Purchases, such as removing ads or unlocking voice packs, are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details; the App only stores locally what you have unlocked. View Google's Privacy Policy

Your Choices and Consent

Where your region requires it, the App shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) on launch. In those regions Firebase Analytics collects nothing until you have agreed through that form; if you decline, or the form cannot be completed, analytics stays off. Where consent is not required, analytics runs anonymously by default. You can reset your advertising ID in your Android settings, and ad personalization can also be limited in your Google account's ad settings.

4. Permissions

The App may request the following permissions to function correctly:

  • WAKE_LOCK: To keep the screen or CPU awake during active timers or clock display.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: To show timer progress and controls in your device's notification area.
  • INTERNET: To load advertisements, send consented analytics, and handle in-app purchase verification.
  • AD_ID (advertising ID): To let Google's advertising and analytics services use your advertising identifier, which you can reset or delete in Android settings.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING: To facilitate in-app purchases.

5. Children's Privacy

Our App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided personal data, please contact us.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at codicium91+dev@gmail.com.
